THE PABNA PARANORMAL ARCHIVES

Regional historical lore collected from eyewitnesses, riverfolk, and declassified 1957 sanatorium files.

1. The Hemayetpur Sanatorium Burial Ground (1957)

Erected upon ancient zamindar cremation lands near the Ichamati River. When the monsoon floodwaters rise, psychiatric staff recorded unidentifiable footsteps ascending from the riverbank directly into Ward 7.

2. The Tarash Zamindar Palace Colonnade

Built with European and Mughal neoclassical masonry. Patient Kumar Birendra Ray etched the 387-arch equation into his silverware, claiming the pillars vibrate during full moons.

3. Chatmohar Shahi Mosque & Masum Khan Kabuli (1582)

The three domed sanctum resonates at an acoustic harmonic matching the low-frequency hum detected by the asylum's tri-field EMF sensors.

4. Paksey Hardinge Bridge Hot-Riveted Trusses (1915)

Spanning the Padma with 16 massive piers. Dr. Rafiqul Haque claimed each hot-riveted steel girder contains encrypted morse telemetry connecting to the sanatorium's morgue slab.
